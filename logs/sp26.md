## Reflection of Gallery Walk 
During our final class of the semester, we took part in a gallery walk, where students displayed their final projects. My final project is called FlipFinder, which is a Flask web application that assists users in searching real estate listings and assessing house flipping opportunities in the market. I worked alongside my friend Kamila for this assignment. In addition to reviewing important detailed and entering parameters like purchase price, renovation expenditures, and resale value, users can search properties by city or ZIP code. After calculating the total cost, ROI, and projected profit, the program offers a color-coded rating (either green, yellow, and red) to evaluate the strength of the flipping deal. 

I really enjoyed exploring and learning about the different projects that my classmates worked on for this assignment. Some of the projects that caught my attention in particular were: a program that creates a personal website once an individual inputs their resume using a drag-and-drop feature, as well as an internship tracker that allows users to look for different organizations, examine open internship positions, and manage their application progress to keep track of their job search journey. Overall, I would say that this gallery walk was an interesting activity as it allowed everyone to witness the usefulness of the skills and knowledge that we acquired throughout the semester. It demonstrated how the fundamentals of programming can be employed to solve diverse real-world challenges.
