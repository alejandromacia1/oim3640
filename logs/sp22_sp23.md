## What I learned this week
We reviewed the differences between GET and POST: 
- GET: Its main purpose is to retrieve/display data. Its data location is in the URL. Example: Loading a page. Flask decorator: @app.get(...)
- POST: Its main purpose is to submit/send data. Its location is in the request body (hidden). Example: Submitting a form. Flask decorator: @app.post(...). 

Furthermore, we learned that by producing HTML pages that might include variables like user input, Flask templates enable us to go beyond returning strings. Hence, this allows for tailored information and enables us to separate the backend functionality from the frontend presentation that is visible to users, making applications and websites more user-friendly and scalable.
