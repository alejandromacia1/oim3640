
# Learning Log - Mini Project 2

## What did I learn during the process of creating this project? 
While creating this second mini project, I was able to broaden and enhance my comprehension of how platforms like VSCode can be utilized for data-driven analysis, as well as text processing. This project gave me the opportunity to engage with a real book, titled Wuthering Heights, and translate its dialogue into quantifiable data by comparing the amount of time (in words) that each of the two protagonists, Heathcliff and Catherine, spent speaking in each of the chapters. During the development and completion of the project, we implemented concepts/techniques such as text extraction, lists, condition statements, loops, functions, word-counting, and dictionaries, amongst others. Furthermore, since this project required multiple portions of code, each of which targeted a distinct function, it was crucial for me to structure the program correctly to make each of the sections more digestible in terms of revision and formatting. This assignment helped me understand that programming has other applications outside of technical analysis. I was able to relate class topics to a contemporary artistic and cultural subject because I selected a topic that truly intrigued me. Overall, I was able to discover how to leverage the tools in class we've learnt about throughout the semester to investigate dialogue dominance, narrative balance in a book. 

## How did AI tools help me during the development of the project. 
One of the biggest challenges I encountered during the development of this project was knowing how to identify which of the characters/who was speaking across the different chapters of the novel. While in some cases, the speaker markers were clear, in multiple sections of the book, the dialogue attribution was not clear or was indirect. As a result of this, I utilized ChatGPT and Claude as additional resources in an effort to help me determine how to tackle these problems efficiently and to clarify particular sections of the code. For instance, AI made it easier for me to consider how to structure the analysis into more understandable functions, how speaker recognition operates, and how conventional expressions might be utilized to retrieve quoted speech.
