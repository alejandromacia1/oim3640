
# Mini Project #3 Proposal

## What I’m building: 
For this project, I am developing a web application that helps users determine the closest MBTA stop given the location or place that is given within the Boston area.

## Why I chose this: 
After my freshman year at Babson, I moved off campus into the city. However, not long after, I got an on-campus job at the skating center as a rink attendant. The majority of my shifts were during the weekends, so I was required to commute back to campus on Fridays and Saturdays, which were usually at times when none of my friends with cars were available to take me (or had to go to Babson’s campus). Additionally, my trips back to the city weren’t always the same. I would finish my shifts during the afternoon, and I would often meet my friends in different parts of the city. This meant that I had to use different T stops and lines almost every time. At the time, I was not familiar with using public transportation, considering that back home the public transportation systems are not as developed nor secure when compared to those in the United States. Therefore, getting comfortable with the different lines and stops was definitely a challenge I managed to overcome.

## Core features:
- Helps you locate the closest MBTA stop. 
- A search tool that allows you to enter a set address and converts the name of the place to coordinates using the MapBox API. 
- With the information provided, the tool displays the name of the stop and provides information about wheelchair accessibility.
